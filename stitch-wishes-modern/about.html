Our Story

Abi's Story

Stitch Wishess started small — a handful of hand-beaded pens made at a kitchen table, inspired by the characters and colors Abi loved most growing up. What began as a hobby turned into a studio built entirely around one idea: every piece should feel like it was made for the person receiving it.

Today the collection has grown to include painted canvases, keychains, car accessories, and night lights, but the process hasn't changed. Every item is still handmade or made to order, one wish at a time, with the same care as that very first pen.

Thank you for supporting a small, independent studio — every order helps keep this story going.
